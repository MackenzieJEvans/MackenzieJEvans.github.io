About

Hello, I'm Mackenzie Evans and I am a student journalist.

I'm from the small town of Tekamah, Nebraska and am currently studying in Lincoln.

I am majoring in Journalism and minoring in Food Science and Technology at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln. I also have previous experience with Advertising and Public Relations and website design/management.

I have been using R for data analysis in both food science and journalism since 2023. I learned SQL independently. I am always excited to learn new skills.

I can best be described as curious. My background in science allows me to apply research and data analytics to my articles.

I look forward to connecting more with the state of Nebraska and listening to everyone's stories.
