How to play

Fuel drops — each one adds a little gas. Clear every drop to finish the level.
Gas station — big refill, and every cone gets knocked over for a few seconds.
Cones, barrels, barricades & signs — they roll out of the Cone Yard and chase you. A hit costs fuel.
Road closed — permanent construction. Find a detour.

Drive with ←↑→↓ or WASD (swipe on phones). Space siren (fire truck only), P pause, M mute. O Street runs off both edges of the map — use it as a shortcut.

Street grid is a stylized take on downtown Lincoln, not to scale.
